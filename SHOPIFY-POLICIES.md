## 1. Return and refund policy

If a piece is not right for you, we offer a **14-day return and exchange window** from the date of delivery.

**1. Eligibility**
- Items must be unworn and in their original condition.
- The piece must be returned with its original pouch and packaging.
- Personalised or made-to-order items are final sale unless there is a manufacturing defect.

**2. How to start a return**
- Email avirenajewels@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 78238 89290 with your order number and the reason for the return.
- We will arrange a courier pickup from your address.

**3. Refunds**
- Once your return reaches us, we inspect it within 2 business days.
- Approved refunds are credited to your original payment method within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.
- You may instead choose store credit for the full value, with no deduction.

**4. Damaged or faulty items**
If your order arrives damaged, email avirenajewels@gmail.com within 48 hours of delivery with photographs. We will send a replacement at no additional cost, or refund you in full.

**5. Your statutory rights**
This policy is in addition to your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. Nothing here limits those rights.

Questions? Contact avirenajewels@gmail.com.

## 2. Shipping policy

**1. Order processing**
- In-stock pieces are dispatched within 1–2 business days (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays).
- You will receive tracking details by email once your order ships.

**2. Delivery within India**
- Estimated delivery: 2–5 business days after dispatch, depending on your location.
- Shipping is free on orders above ₹1,999. A flat ₹99 applies below that.
- Delivery timelines are estimates, not guarantees; couriers can be delayed by weather, strikes or regional restrictions.

**3. International delivery**
- Estimated delivery: 7–14 business days after dispatch.
- Shipping is calculated at checkout.
- Any customs duties, import taxes or handling fees charged by the destination country are payable by the recipient and are not included in the price you pay us.

**4. Lost or delayed parcels**
If tracking has not updated for 7 business days, contact avirenajewels@gmail.com and we will open an investigation with the courier. If a parcel is confirmed lost in transit, we will replace it or refund you in full.

**5. Address accuracy**
Please check your delivery address at checkout. We cannot reroute a parcel once it has been dispatched, and re-delivery of a parcel returned due to an incorrect address may incur a further shipping charge.
