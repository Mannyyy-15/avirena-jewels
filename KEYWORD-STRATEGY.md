# Avirena — Keyword & AEO Strategy

Research date: 5 September 2026. Based on live SERP analysis of the Indian
anti-tarnish / fashion jewellery market.

---

## The core finding

**The market competes on "anti-tarnish", not "dailywear".**

Every competitor ranking for your buyer's queries — Salty, Aashirs, Pipa Bella,
Kanti Kundan, TheJewelBox, Rubans, Nithilah — leads with *anti-tarnish* or
*waterproof*. "Dailywear" is how you described yourselves; "anti-tarnish" is
what customers type. Titles have been retargeted accordingly.

**Your ₹499 price sits in the busiest band.** Competitors cluster at ₹199–899,
with "under 500" being a heavily-searched qualifier. That is why `under-999`
is a product tag and why an "under ₹999" collection is recommended below.

---

## The opening nobody has taken

Every page ranking for brass-jewellery *informational* queries is a
US or international brand — Dea Dia, Parkdale Brass, Galleria Armadoro,
Stylr, AJLuxe, Zearrow.

**No Indian brand owns these answers.** That is your fastest route to
traffic, because:

1. Informational queries are far less contested than product queries
2. Indian buyers have India-specific concerns (humidity, monsoon, sweat)
   that US content does not address
3. These are exactly the queries AI answer engines synthesise and cite

Your four guides already target this. Their metadata now carries India
signals (humidity, sweat, Indian sizing) to separate them from US results.

### Long-tail gaps — worth writing next

Ranked by (search intent × how easily you can answer honestly):

1. **"jewellery that doesn't turn skin green india"** — high purchase intent,
   directly answered by your existing guide. Needs its own section or page.
2. **"anti tarnish jewellery in monsoon / humid weather"** — genuinely
   India-specific, zero competition, and a real customer worry.
3. **"jewellery for sensitive ears india"** — your surgical steel posts are a
   real differentiator; nickel-free is a medical concern, not a style one.
4. **"gifting jewellery for wife/sister under 1000"** — festival and occasion
   driven, spikes around Raksha Bandhan, Karwa Chauth, Diwali.
5. **"how to store jewellery so it doesn't tarnish"** — care intent, links
   naturally to your care guide and back to product pages.
6. **"is brass jewellery safe to wear daily"** — safety intent, high trust value.

## AEO — being cited by AI answer engines

Already in place:
- `robots.txt` explicitly allows GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot,
  ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended
- Every page is prerendered, so crawlers that do not execute JS still get
  full content
- `llms.txt` states verified brand facts with no marketing inflation
- Guides open with a direct answer in the first 1–2 sentences — the passage
  format answer engines quote

What makes Avirena *citable* rather than just crawlable: the guides state
plainly that brass **can** react with skin. Honest answers get cited;
marketing copy gets skipped. Do not soften this.

## What NOT to do

- **Do not claim "waterproof"** unless it is literally true. Competitors use
  it loosely; a false durability claim is an ASCI problem and a returns problem.
- **Do not add review schema** until a real review system exists. Fabricated
  ratings were removed for exactly this reason.
- **Do not target "gold jewellery"** or precious-metal keywords. Wrong
  audience, wrong price expectation, and misleading.
- **Do not keyword-stuff titles again.** The originals were 101 characters
  and truncated in every SERP.
